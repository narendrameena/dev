Figure 3 | Single-cell versus single-nucleus measurement of the panel. All comparisons are made within matched cell types (>=30 cells in both assays; n = 33 cell types), because the two assays recover different cell-type proportions and pooled means would confound assay with composition. a, Mean log-normalised expression per gene and cell type; dashed line is identity. b, Mean difference per gene across matched cell types, 11 most scRNA-biased and 11 most snRNA-biased. c, Detection rate per gene and cell type. d, Difference per gene and cell type, rows ordered by mean bias. Source: Q5_sc_vs_sn_by_celltype.csv, Q5_sc_vs_sn_per_gene.csv.
